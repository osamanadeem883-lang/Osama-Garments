This folder is the public website.


THE EASY WAY
------------
Open admin.html (one folder up), go to Website, and press
"Put the whole website online". When it asks for a folder,
pick THIS folder - the folder itself, not the files in it.

Everything here goes up in one go, and your products and
prices come from the admin as you last left them.


THE OLD WAY
-----------
Drag everything in here onto github.com and the site updates.
Full instructions: "HOW TO UPDATE THE WEBSITE.txt", one folder up.


Never put admin.html on the website. The publish button leaves
it out on purpose.
