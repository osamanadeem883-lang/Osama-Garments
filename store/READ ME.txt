THE WHOLE SHOP IN ONE FILE
==========================

index.html in this folder is the entire website.

  the layout, the styling, the code,
  every product and every line of text,
  and the four header photos, all inside it.

Nothing else is needed. No images folder. No script.js.
No style.css. Put this one file on your domain and the
shop works.


PUTTING IT ON A DOMAIN
----------------------
Whatever host you buy (Hostinger, Namecheap, cPanel, GitHub
Pages - any of them), the job is the same:

   upload index.html into the public folder
   (usually called  public_html  or  htdocs  or the repo root)

That is it. Your address then shows the shop.


ABOUT THE ADMIN
---------------
admin.html still works the normal way, in the "Garments store"
folder, with index.html there. Keep using it as you do now.

When you publish from the admin it writes store-data.js.
If you ever drop that store-data.js next to THIS one file,
it takes over and your newer products and prices show. The
header photos keep working either way.

admin.html must NEVER go on the website. Anyone who finds
the address can open it and change your prices.


WHEN THINGS CHANGE
------------------
This file is a snapshot. If you change products or text in
the admin, ask me and I will rebuild it, or just publish
store-data.js next to it.
